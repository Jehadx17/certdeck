# Delete your Darb account

This page explains how to delete your account in **Darb (درب)**, the study app by **[COMPANY]**, and all the data linked
to it. Published at https://darbedu.sa/delete-account · [العربية](https://darbedu.sa/ar/delete-account) [OWNER: final URLs]

## 1. Cancel your subscription first
Deleting your account **does not cancel** a Darb Plus subscription. The store keeps billing you until you cancel there:
- **iPhone:** Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Darb → Cancel subscription
- **Android:** Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Darb → Cancel

## 2. Delete in the app (fastest)
1. Open Darb.
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete my account**.
3. Confirm.

Your account is deleted from our servers at once, the app removes your Darb data from that phone, and you are signed out
everywhere. On your other phones, uninstall Darb to remove the copy kept there for offline study.

## 3. Delete without the app
Email **support@darbedu.sa** from the email address of your Darb account, with the subject **"Delete my account"**.
- If you signed in with Apple and chose **Hide My Email**, write from the email address your Apple account forwards to,
  and include the relay address. You can find it in your Apple account settings, under
  Sign in with Apple → Darb.
- If you write from a different address, we will ask you to confirm the request from your account email before we
  delete anything.

**Guardians** of students under 18 can use the link in the consent email ("Withdraw consent and delete"), or email us from
the address they confirmed with.

For email requests, we reply when the deletion is done.

## What is deleted
- Your account: name, email, Google or Apple account identifier, birth year
- Your guardian's email and the consent record [LAWYER: unless a minimal consent record must be kept, see the privacy
  policy, section 10]
- All your study data: settings, sessions, review history, your cards, saved AI explanations and feedback
- Your uploaded PDFs and photos, and everything made from them
- Sign-in access on all your phones. We also revoke Darb's Sign in with Apple access, and delete your customer record
  at RevenueCat (our subscription service).

## When
- **In the app:** at once.
- **By email:** within **30 days** of your request (or of your confirmation, if we had to ask for one).
- **Backups:** our encrypted backups age out within **35 more days**. Until then they cannot be used to bring your
  account back, except to recover from a system failure, and any restored copy is deleted again.

## What may be kept
- **Billing records** that Apple, Google and RevenueCat must keep under their own terms and the law. We cannot delete
  those. Ask the store if you want to know more.
- **Data that no longer identifies you:** the cost figures of past AI use, and the text of mistake reports you sent, are
  kept without any link to you.

Nothing else is kept.
