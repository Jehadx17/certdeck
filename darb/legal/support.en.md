## Frequently asked questions

**1. How do I sign in?**
With your Google account or your Apple account. Darb has no passwords. Use the same one every time: signing in with
Google and then with Apple creates two separate Darb accounts. If you are under 13, the Google or Apple account must be
one that your parent manages.

**2. Can my parent or guardian see or delete my data?**
Yes. Your guardian can email support@darbedu.sa to see, correct or delete your data. You can also delete your account
yourself in the app: **Settings → Account → Delete my account**.

**3. Can I study without internet?**
Yes. Downloaded packs and your progress stay on your phone. Darb saves your progress to your account when you are back
online. Before signing out, connect once, so that nothing on the phone is lost.

**4. How do I subscribe, cancel or restore Darb Plus?**
Subscribe from **Upgrade** in the app; you pay through the App Store or Google Play. To cancel, go to **Settings →
Subscription → Manage or cancel**, which opens the store page. Darb Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid
for. On a new phone, sign in with the same Darb account and tap **Restore purchases**.

**5. Can I get a refund?**
Payments go through Apple or Google, so refunds follow their rules. On iPhone, request one at
https://reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, request it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history),
or see Google Play's refund help. We are happy to help by email.

**6. A question or an answer is wrong. What do I do?**
Tap the flag at the top of the question, or **Report this answer** under an AI answer. We check every report and tell
you in the app when it is fixed.

**7. Why did the AI help stop?**
Each account has a daily number of AI requests and a monthly AI allowance. The daily count resets the next day; the
monthly allowance comes back on the 1st of each month, and the app shows the date. Darb Plus has higher limits.
Studying always works, even when the AI allowance is used up.

**8. How do I delete my account?**
In the app: **Settings → Account → Delete my account**. Without the app, see https://darbedu.sa/delete-account.
Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in the store first.

**9. Which devices does Darb support?**
Android 7.0 or later and iOS 13 or later [OWNER: and iPad, if supported]. On iOS 13, and on Android phones without
Google Play services, a security check that paid packs need is not available, so Darb Plus question banks can't be
downloaded there. Update to iOS 14 or later before you subscribe. [OWNER: see the decision note in the release
checklist]

**10. What happens to my data?**
We keep only what Darb needs: your name and email from Google or Apple, your birth year, and your study progress. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell data. AI features send the question and
your answer to Anthropic's AI without your name or email. Details are in the
[privacy policy](https://darbedu.sa/privacy).
