This policy covers the Darb (درب) app (`sa.darbedu.app`) on Android and iOS and our web pages at darbedu.sa.

## In short (for students)
- You sign in with Google or Apple. We get your name and email from them, and you tell us your birth year.
- If you are under 18, we do not ask for a guardian's email. A guardian can write to us to see, correct or delete your data.
- We save your study progress so that it stays with you on every phone you use.
- When you use AI features, the question, your answer or your uploaded pages go to Anthropic's AI. Your name and email
  are never sent with them.
- No ads. No tracking. We never sell your data.
- You can delete your account in the app at any time: **Settings → Account → Delete my account**.

## 5. What we never do
- We do not show ads or use any data for advertising.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not collect the advertising ID.
- We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.
- We do not send marketing emails. The only emails we send are replies to your messages.
- Our web pages use no tracking or advertising cookies.

## 6. AI features
- **What they are:** explanations of questions, grading of your practice answers, re-teaching of cards you keep missing,
  and turning your uploaded PDFs and photos into cards and practice (subscribers).
- **Who runs the AI:** Anthropic PBC (the Claude model), in the United States.
- **What is sent:** only what the feature needs: the text of the card or question, your answer or question about it, and
  for uploads, the pages you uploaded.
- **What is never sent:** your name, email, or account ID.
- **What Anthropic does with it:** it uses the data only to answer us. It does not use it to train its models. It
  deletes it within 30 days, unless it is needed to investigate misuse or the law requires otherwise.
- **Your choice:** the app asks for your consent before the first AI use and names Anthropic. If you don't agree, you can
  still study; only the AI features stay off.
- **Shared explanations:** an explanation of a question from our study packs may be saved and shown to other students who
  ask about the same question. These saved explanations contain no personal data.
- **Limits:** AI answers are written by a computer, not a teacher, and can be wrong. They are labelled "AI", and each one
  has "Report this answer". AI grading in Darb is practice only and never decides a grade, admission or certificate.
- **Safety:** every AI request carries safety instructions that keep answers on studying and suitable for students aged
  12 and over, and students cannot chat freely with the AI. More detail: [AI and minors statement](https://darbedu.sa/ai-and-minors).

## 7. Students under 18
- Darb is for students aged **12 and over**. We ask for your birth year only, and we do not create an account for anyone
  younger than 12.
- **Under 13:** Google and Apple require that a parent manages the account you sign in with.
- **Under 18:** we do not ask for a guardian's email and there is no guardian approval step. Your birth year is the only
  age information we hold.
- **What guardians can do:** write to support@darbedu.sa to see, correct or delete their child's data. Students can also
  delete the account themselves in the app (Settings → Account → Delete my account).
- **Purchases:** subscriptions are bought through the Apple or Google account on the phone. For minors that account is
  usually managed by a parent (Ask to Buy or Family Link). [LAWYER: decide whether a minor may enter the subscription

- We do not sell children's data, show ads to anyone, or use any data for advertising.

## 9. Transfers outside Saudi Arabia
We store your account and study data on servers run by Contabo in Germany, and our backups (encrypted) in Cloudflare R2
storage restricted to the European Union. When you use AI features, the study content you send is processed by
Anthropic in the United States and other countries where Anthropic runs its models. RevenueCat and [Sentry, the email
provider] also process data abroad, as listed in section 8. These transfers are needed to provide the app. We protect
them with the Standard Contractual Clauses issued by the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA), we assessed their risks
before starting, and we send each provider only what it needs.

If Saudi rules change to require storing personal data inside the Kingdom, we will move it and update this policy.

## 11. Your rights
Under the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), you have the right to:
- **be told** how we use your data (this policy);
- **access** your data and **get a copy** in a readable format;
- **correct** data that is wrong or incomplete;
- **have it destroyed** when we no longer need it, or when you ask for it to be deleted;
- **withdraw consent** at any time. This does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew.

**How to use them:** delete your account in the app (**Settings → Account → Delete my account**) or on
https://darbedu.sa/delete-account. For anything else, email support@darbedu.sa from your account email (guardians: from
the email they write from, with the student's account email). We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

**Our response time:** within **30 days**. If we need up to 30 more days, we will tell you why before the first 30 end.

**Complaints:** please write to us first. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Saudi Data and
AI Authority (SDAIA) through its National Data Governance Platform (https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa). [LAWYER: confirm the
complaint channel and any deadline]

## 12. Security
- All connections use encryption (TLS).
- Sign-in tokens are stored only as scrambled hashes, and each phone has its own token, which ends when you sign out.
- Backups are encrypted (AES-256) before they leave our server.
- Paid study packs are encrypted and contain no personal data.
- Rate limits and app-integrity checks block automated abuse.
- Access to production systems is limited to [named staff] using [two-factor authentication]. [OWNER: confirm]
- **If a breach happens:** we notify SDAIA within 72 hours where it could harm you, and we notify you without undue delay.

No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is less to lose.
